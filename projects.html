Market Research • BFSI PRJ-01

Study on the Role of Social Media Marketing in the BFSI Sector

Analyzed how social media marketing influences customer trust and engagement in the BFSI sector. Based on a survey of 100 respondents, it found that while platforms like Instagram, LinkedIn, and Facebook are widely used, trust and transparency issues still impact customer perception. The study suggests improving authenticity and transparency to enhance engagement.

Mentor Mr. Yash Varma
Timeline 06 Jun, 2025 – 06 Aug, 2025
Sample Size 100 Survey Respondents
Core Channels Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook
Core Finding

"While digital banking channels have widespread adoption, long-term customer trust and engagement require clear authenticity and transparent communication."

Brand Governance & Strategy PRJ-02

Study of Challenges in Managing Multi-Brand Social Media Portfolios

Focused on understanding the challenges companies face while managing multiple brands on different social media platforms. Evaluated how each brand maintains its unique identity, creates engaging content, and handles audience interactions. Gained practical insights into social media strategy, content planning, and performance analysis, while improving creativity, coordination, and analytical thinking.

Mentor Mr. Harjeet Singh
Domain Social Media Strategy & Content Planning
Key Competencies Creativity, Coordination, Analytical Thinking
Strategic Learning

"Maintaining distinct brand identities across shared portfolio channels requires structured content planning and responsive audience coordination."
